## Why this task

Traffic-sign recognition is a safety-critical piece of driver-assistance perception, and GTSRB is the standard German benchmark for it. I wanted to own the whole loop an ML engineer owns, not just call a pretrained model.

## Model

Inputs are 32×32 RGB. There are three convolutional blocks (32→32, 64→64, then 128 channels), each with BatchNorm and ReLU, followed by a classifier head of 2048 → 256 → 43 with dropout of 0.4 and 0.3. That's **675,659 parameters**, small enough to train on a laptop CPU in 8 epochs.

## What I'd do for production

97.7% on a clean test set is a starting point, not a safety case. Next would be a **per-class confusion analysis** (speed-limit signs are easily confused with each other), robustness tests under blur, glare and night conditions, and **quantisation** to check accuracy and latency on embedded hardware.
